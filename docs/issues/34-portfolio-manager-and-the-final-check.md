# 34: The Portfolio Manager, the Playbook and the final check

**What to build:** A Portfolio Manager Agent starts from each Rule plan and changes it within its Book's Playbook, writing a Decision record with the evidence for each change. A final check in code accepts a change only inside the Mandate, inside its Lever's range and allowed by the Book's Judgement mode, with every cited figure found in the Run's inputs. A failing change is dropped, and the whole Book is then checked as the Order list would leave it. If it fails, the Rule plan goes out instead, but only once it passes the same check. See the stocks Pipeline PRD's Portfolio Manager and Check Steps, "The Market condition and the Playbook", and ADR 0003.

**Repos:** agents, ash_new

**Blocked by:** 33: Paper Books trade their Order lists; 46: Market condition and the exposure level

**Status:** done, but for the dossiers (issue 37) among the Portfolio Manager's inputs, and its first Run on real data: no strategy is in Paper yet (see "Not done")

**Waiting on Saransh:** the Playbook's ranges. Until then the placeholders are exposure floors of 80%, 50% and 0%, sizes from 0.5× to 1×, timing up to 5 sessions and rebalance under 2% of the Book.

- [x] Each Book's config holds its Playbook beside its Mandate, under a version as the Mandate's is: a range for each Lever (exposure, selection, size, timing, trim and rebalance).
- [x] Each Book has a Judgement mode (plan only, subtract only or Playbook), kept in the app database with every change, its date and the Answer that approved it. Real Books start in subtract only, paper Books in Playbook.
- [x] `pd plan` writes each Book's Pool: the names any strategy with a Sleeve in it bought or signalled to buy in the last 2 months, from their live tracks.
- [x] A Portfolio Manager Agent runs on Claude Code, with Codex as fallback, read-only in the Run folder. Its inputs are:
  - the Rule plans and the dossiers;
  - each Book's Playbook, Judgement mode, Market condition, exposure level and Pool;
  - the Data gate's verdict and the Health Agent's diagnosis;
  - the Stages and its Journal.
- [x] Its output is checked against a schema:
  - per Book, its exposure level for the day and its changes from the Rule plan;
  - a Decision record for each change and for the exposure level: the Rule plan's order, what goes out instead, the Lever and Playbook line, each figure cited by input file, security and field with its value, what is expected, and the session it is judged on;
  - the Journal's new entries;
  - the Brief's commentary per Book.
- [x] `pd check` accepts each change only if:
  - the Book's Judgement mode allows it: none in plan only; exposure cuts, dropped buys, trims, waits and rebalance skips in subtract only; also selection swaps within a Sleeve and sizes up to the range's top in Playbook;
  - it is inside its Lever's range, and a bought name is in the Pool;
  - its Decision record is complete and every cited figure matches the Run's inputs;
  - it doesn't drop or delay any of the Rule plan's strategy exits or Disaster stops.

  It drops each change that fails, with its reason.
- [x] `pd check` then checks the whole Book as the Order list would leave it, since changes that pass one by one can break a limit together. It checks:
  - each position and sector against its cap;
  - the stocks held against the exposure level;
  - the buys against the cash left after what open orders reserve.
- [x] If that check fails, or the Portfolio Manager failed, the Rule plan goes out instead, but only once it passes the same whole-Book check. If the Rule plan fails it too, only its exits and Disaster stops go out, with a To-do. The Brief says which applied and why.
- [x] `pd check` treats the Portfolio Manager's output as failed if it was made from a Book state, Mandate version or Playbook version that is no longer current.
- [x] Each accepted change is recorded as an Override, with the Rule plan's order and its Decision record beside it.
- [x] The Journal is the Portfolio Manager's Memory. Save state applies it, and it carries what is still open.
- [x] The Brief shows each Book's Judgement mode, its commentary, and every change with its Decision record in plain sentences, plus each dropped change and why.
- [x] Moving a Book's Judgement mode, or changing a Playbook range, is an Answer on a proposal in the Brief.
- [x] Tests:
  - **ash_new:** `pd check` accepts no change in plan only mode; accepts an exposure cut, a dropped buy, a trim and a wait in subtract only, and rejects a swap or a size above 1× there; accepts a swap to a Pool name in Playbook mode and rejects one outside the Pool; rejects a change outside its range, without a complete Decision record, or citing a figure not in the inputs; rejects a change that drops an exit; drops a failing change and keeps the rest; catches two changes that each pass but together break a cap; falls back to the Rule plan when what remains breaks the Mandate, and sends only exits and stops when the Rule plan fails too; refuses an output made from an old Book state or Playbook version. The Pool covers exactly 2 months of the live tracks.
  - **agents:** the Portfolio Manager gets its inputs; it falls back to Codex; after both Workers fail, the Rule plan goes out with a note; the Brief shows dropped changes.

**Notes:**

- **The Playbook** was already in each Book's config beside its Mandate, under a version (issue 32): an exposure floor per Market condition, sizes from `size_min` to `size_max`, `timing_sessions` and `rebalance_under`, at the PRD's placeholders. Selection and trim have no number: a swap is to a Pool name in the same Sleeve for no more than the buy it drops, and a trim is short of an exit. A range Saransh approves later (`pd judgement approve`) is kept in the app database, not written back into config/books.yaml: the Playbook in force is config's with each approved range, under its own version, which every paper plan names, so a plan made under the old one is no longer current.
- **Judgement modes** (`pdash.judgement`; migration 0017: `judgement_changes`, `judgement_proposals`, `overrides`): each Book's real and paper sides apart, each change with its day, when, who approved it and why. A side with no change is in its first mode: subtract only for a real Book, Playbook for a paper Book. Real Books have no Order lists until issue 42, so only the paper side is used yet.
- **Proposals and Answers:** `pd judgement propose BOOK --mode M [--paper]` or `--playbook NAME=VALUE` records a proposal; `pd judgement show` lists each Book's modes, its Playbook in force and the proposals waiting; the Brief shows each proposal with Approve and Reject buttons, and the Answers Step applies the Answer with `pd judgement approve` or `reject` (item `judgement:<id>`). Nothing proposes yet but Saransh by hand: the override scorecard (issue 40) and Lessons (issue 41) will.
- **`pd plan`** gives each paper Book's plan what the Portfolio Manager judges by and the final check holds it to: its Judgement mode, the Playbook in force, the **Pool**, each holding on the last close (Sleeve, shares, close, value, sector, RS Rating, Disaster stop, whether it may be sold today), the cash open orders reserve, the buys waiting (`waits`, each with its last session, close and limit), and each order's sector and role in its Sleeve (entry, add, exit, trim or stop). The Pool is each name a strategy with a Sleeve in the Book (not Retired) bought or signalled to buy (an entry, or a resize up) on its live track, signalled on a close in the 2 months to the last close.
- **The Portfolio Manager Agent** (`agents/portfolio-manager/`; Claude Code, Codex as fallback, read-only in the Run folder) runs in a new Step after Plan. Its inputs are the Rule plans (`plan.json`, with the Data gate's verdict and each market's condition and exposure level), the Book states, the Stages, the Health Agent's diagnosis (null when the Lake was healthy), the dossiers (null until issue 37) and its Journal. Its output, checked against its schema: per paper Book, the plan it judged (by its ids and versions), its exposure level for the day with a Decision record, its changes (drop, resize a new position, swap a strategy's buy, wait, release, trim or skip), each with a Decision record, and the Brief's commentary; then the Journal's new entries and everything still open. A Decision record cites each figure by input file (`plan.json`, `book-states.json`, `stages.json`, `health.json`), what it is about, the field and its value. It skips itself unless the Plan Step succeeded with a paper plan; failing on both Workers fails the Step and is recorded.
- **The final check** (`judgement/final_check.py`, run by `pd check --judgement`): the Check Step hands the answer over as `judgement.json` (`contract/judgement.schema.json`), or why there is none. Each change is accepted only if the Judgement mode allows it (none in plan only; the subtracting ones in subtract only; swaps and sizes up to the top in Playbook), the plan isn't in Hold mode, it is inside its Lever's range, a name it buys is in the Pool, it changes no exit or Disaster stop, and its Decision record is complete (judged on a session after the plan's) with every figure found in the Run's inputs (`judgement/inputs.py`: an object about the security holding the field at the value, a number to the places it is cited to). Each failing change is dropped with why. An exposure cut then works as the exposure level does: no stock buys while the stocks are over it and the weakest trimmed first, else the buys cut to the room. The whole Book as the list would leave it is checked against the position and sector caps (for what it buys), the exposure level (when it buys stocks), and the cash less what open orders reserve, with the sales' money. If that fails, or the judgement failed, said nothing of the Book, or was made from another plan, Book state, Mandate or Playbook version, the Rule plan goes out once it passes; else only its exits and Disaster stops, with a To-do the Check Step raises. The rules-only paper Book's list is always its Rule plan.
- **Overrides:** each accepted change of a list that went out with them is recorded with its kind, Lever, the Rule plan's order, the orders it made, its Decision record and the session it is judged on. A wait is open until a release buys it or its last session passes (`waits` in the next plans); a list changed by the judgement has its own id, so a re-run with another judgement supersedes it and no Override is recorded twice.
- **The Journal** is the Portfolio Manager's Memory (`journal`): a new stocks Save state Step, after Check, adds the answer's entries under the Run's id (keeping the latest 60 Runs') and replaces what is still open, applying it, or queuing it for `agents memory` when Saransh edited it after the Agent read it. A dry run's, or a failed Agent's, changes nothing.
- **The Brief:** a Portfolio Manager section, after the plans, shows for each paper Book its Judgement mode, the commentary, what went out and why not the changes, the exposure level for the day, each accepted change with its Decision record in plain sentences, and each change the final check dropped with why; or that the Portfolio Manager failed and the Rule plans went out. Then each Book's Judgement modes and Playbook. Proposals waiting have their own section with Answer buttons.
- **Pipeline order:** answers, broker-inbox, marketsmith, lake, stages, health, plan, **portfolio-manager**, check, **save-state**, brief.

- **Code review** (Standards and Spec, in parallel). Fixed:
  - **Spec:** a swap of gold's buy could buy a stock in gold's Sleeve, outside the exposure level's count; gold's buy can't be swapped now. A resize multiplied any buy's shares, though the size Lever is a multiple of the strategy's size: only a new position's buy (role `entry`) is resized now. The figure lookup was loose: a rounded figure matched far from its value (a cited 1 matched 0.55), a Book's code matched any of its orders' prices, and a cited `true` matched 1; now a number must be within 1% too, a security's figures are found only under its own id, and a boolean only as a boolean. The exposure level's Decision record went unchecked when the level was kept; now its gaps are said in the Brief (the plan's level stands). A judgement not in its schema's shape raised in `pd check`; now that Book's Rule plan goes out, with why.
  - **Standards:** the Brief's header comment had overlong lines; `Recorded.list_id` (a row id) shared its name with the list's id string (now `order_list_id`); ash_new's glossary now says a buy made to wait isn't a Waiting order; the Lever list is one constant; the Portfolio Manager Step formats its log with `percent`; an unused Override query and an unused flag are gone; the three order shapes the final check makes are one helper.
  - Left as judgement calls: "skip" and "drop" beside the glossary's skipped orders and vetoes, the plan kinds and change kinds as strings, the bare `paper` flag for a Judgement mode's side, the long parameter lists of `judging`, `final_list` and `_not_current`, and the whole-Book check holding the position and sector caps only for what the list buys (as the Book engine does: a holding grown over its cap by its price isn't sold, so the Rule plan never fails on it), and the exposure level only when the list buys stocks.
