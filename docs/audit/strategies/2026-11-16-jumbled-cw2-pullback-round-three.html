1. How it was judged

Cells are Sharpe / CAGR / max drawdown, in local currency, after each market's costs, cash earning nothing. Sharpe is on daily returns over the market's risk-free rate: India 6%, the US and Hong Kong 3%. The three periods are the study's, and they don't overlap:

  • 2016–2026: 16 August 2016 to 14 August 2026, the selection window;
  • 2013–2016: 1 January 2013 to 15 August 2016;
  • 2007–2012: on early panels built from this machine's lake, as the frozen ones are.

Each change went over the shipped strategy, one at a time. It counted as better where its CAGR beat the shipped strategy's in all three periods and its Sharpe in at least two. It was then taken only if the settings beside it held too. With eight to sixteen holdings, a single good setting is mostly which stocks happened to get a slot. All three periods were used in choosing, here and in the study, so none is an untouched test.

This machine's US runs differ from the study's. India's and Hong Kong's agree with its record to a tenth of a point. The US's are 2 to 6 points a year apart: us-jumbled makes 14.4% over 2016–2026 here against 16.4% there, and us-jumbled-selected 10.4% against 16.8%. The two lakes' US universes or fundamentals differ, which this round didn't trace. Every comparison here is with this lake's own run of the shipped strategy.

6. What didn't hold

  • The up-day ranking made both Jumbleds worse. Without it, India's CW2 Pullback made 16.8% over 2016–2026 at a 38% drawdown, against 19.8% at 24%, so the study's 0.2 there stands.
  • The daily SuperTrend entry gate cost India's two pullbacks 2 to 7 points a year in every period. It buys after the bounce.
  • A 30% trailing stop changed almost nothing, since the SuperTrend sells first.
  • A slower SuperTrend (14 bars) helped in one or two periods and cost the rest.
  • The pullback's stop 5% under the average was worse in India.
  • Watching 52 weeks after the breakout was worse over 2016–2026 and 2013–2016 everywhere.
  • For India's Jumbled selected, the plain CW2 Pullback and US Jumbled, no change held over all three periods.

10. How much to trust it

  • 136 variants in 408 runs, on top of the study's 1,385. Some of every gain here is luck. Taking only changes whose neighbours held guards against that, but doesn't cure it.
  • The only fresh evidence doesn't back the changes. Over the 12 weeks no choice saw, three of the four did slightly worse than before. That's too short to undo them.
  • The US's gain is the largest and the most in doubt, given that the two lakes' US runs disagree.
  • The 12 November record's limits all stand: survivorship in the universe, a week seen two sessions late, few holdings, the markets' cost presets rather than the brokers', and no Book backtest.
