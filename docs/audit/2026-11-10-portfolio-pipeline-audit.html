Portfolio pipeline audit

10–11 November 2026 · the stocks Pipeline (this repo) and ash_new (backend/portfolio-dashboard)

Verdict. The Pipeline is sound and ran end to end on 10 November. Its weakness was not in the plumbing: the Books as configured earned about half of what their strategies earn alone, and a few data checks had gaps. Five of the seven changes proposed are now in the working trees of both repos (nothing is committed). One was built, tested and taken out again because it tested worse. One, the US and Hong Kong Books, is a decision for you and is laid out in section 6.

1. How it was measured

Every figure below is from ash_new's own Book backtest (pd book-backtest and the same engine called from scripts), on the Lake as refreshed on 10 November 2026. Each is given as CAGR / max drawdown over two windows:

  • the range: 9 November 2016 to 9 November 2026;
  • the holdout: 1 January 2013 to 15 August 2016, the window ash_new's research held out.

The Books are the three as they stand: India (Super 30 OK tuned and CW2 selected), the US (CW2 selected and Super 30 OK tuned) and Hong Kong (the same pair), each at Real-full, with 10% gold.

How far to trust the numbers. Each Book is one path through eight to twenty positions, so small changes move results by two or three points either way. The settings below were chosen after looking at both windows, so the holdout is no longer a clean test of them. Read the direction and the size of an effect, not its last digit. No backtest here includes capital gains tax or brokerage above ₹0 and $0 (section 8).

3. What was changed, and what it did

The Mandate, second version (7d9f896c659f)

  • A Disaster stop is never nearer than 10% past its strategy's own trailing stop (disaster_stop_past_trail: 0.10). India CW2's goes from 20% to 40% under its entry; Super 30's from 30% to 35%. Hong Kong CW2's stays at 50%, which was already past its trail.
  • A Disaster stop stays where its entry set it (raise_disaster_stops: false). The Order list no longer asks you to raise stops on Saturdays.

A Mandate with the first version's numbers still hashes to the first version (f99c88f13b5b): both settings default to the old behaviour, so a plan made before the change is not mistaken for one made after.

The Market condition

  • Distribution days make a Correction only while the index is under its 50-day average. Seven of them above it are Under pressure (80%), not a Correction (40%); five alone change nothing.
  • Hong Kong's breadth and volume are measured over its liquid securities: those trading at least HK$20M a day on average over the 20 sessions before. India and the US keep their index members.

4. The breaker: tried, not kept

The first breaker stops a Book's buys at a 10% drawdown until it recovers above 5%. A Book whose stops have sold most of what it held recovers slowly, which is why it was locked out for a year or more at a time. The proposal was a second way out, when the market returns to an Uptrend. It was built into the engine and measured on top of the two changes above. It was then removed, because it tested worse:

With the stops and the Market condition changed, the breaker is no longer what holds India back (with or without it: 22.4% against 22.5%), and it still protects Hong Kong and the US from drawdowns of 19% to 21%. No way out helped all three Books, and the holdout was the same under every one. The breakers are unchanged.

5. Data safety

FindingWhat was done
The index was not gated. A missing index bar was carried forward by the panels and warned only after five sessions, yet the index sets the exposure level and the strategies' sell-everything exit.Fixed. The Data gate fails when a market's index has no bar on the Lake's latest session.
US and Hong Kong prices had one source. The only check of a single bar was a move over 40%; India alone was compared with MarketSmith.Fixed. After the plans are made, pd cross-check compares the closes they rest on with a second source: NSE's own bhavcopy and index file for India, Tencent's daily bars for the US, Hong Kong and their indices. A close over 1% away quarantines the security, or the index (which fails the gate), and the plans are made again. On 10 November's plan it compared 23 closes and all agreed, in 8 seconds.
The second source itself failed quietly once, in testing. Asked for a US name without its exchange, Tencent returns only the latest bar, so a check made while New York was trading found no bar of the last session and compared nothing.Fixed. US names are asked for by their exchange, which returns several days (19 of 19 compared while New York was open). A source that answers but has none of a market's closes is now a To-do in the Brief.
Quarantines only piled up. 1,046 were open on 813 securities; about 21 were added a day and none lifted unless you answered.Fixed. A Quarantine lifts once the Health report no longer raises its flag, unless a Correction for the security is waiting or its market could not be checked.
The Health Agent re-read every flag daily. 513 flags, none on a holding or Pool name, 9.5 million tokens and 8 minutes, with Codex out of quota.Fixed. It is called for new flags, flags on a security the Books hold or may trade, and securities the Lake no longer prices that have not been looked up. It is given those, and told how many were left out.
The Agent could describe a bad bar but not propose its Correction: its output schema had no bar kind, as it said itself in the 10 November diagnosis.Fixed. The schema now allows it.
A quarantined holding cannot be sold on a strategy exit, only by its Disaster stop.Open. By design, but it bites exactly when a held name collapses. See section 8.

The second source is unofficial for the US and Hong Kong, as Yahoo is. A symbol or a day it does not have is listed, not flagged, and a source that cannot be reached is a To-do in the Brief and holds nothing up.

The alternative: the index under a trend rule

Hold an S&P 500 ETF from the session after the index has closed above its 200-day average five sessions in a row; sell on the session after it closes 3% under that average; out of it, sit in the cash fund. Keep the Book's 10% in gold. This is the same market-trend rule the strategies already use, applied to the index itself.

Rough tax: for an Indian resident, as config/regions.yaml assumes, a gain realised within 24 months at 30% and later at 12.5%; losses not set off; the holding at the end taxed as if sold. Cess and surcharge are left out. It is a guide to the ordering, not a tax computation.

What the table says:

  • The trend rule beats today's US Book in both windows (12.5% against 10.8%, 9.9% against 4.6%) with about one trade a year in one ETF, against hundreds of trades in single stocks.
  • The trend rule does not beat simply holding the index. It gives up about 2.5 points of CAGR a year and roughly halves the drawdown (−16% against −31%). After tax the gap widens to almost four points, because every exit within 24 months is taxed at the slab rate.
  • The 3% band is what makes the rule workable. Without it the rule trades 43 times instead of 17 and loses another 1.4 points. The 150-, 200- and 250-day versions land within about a point of each other, so the result does not hang on the exact average.
  • By year, the rule matched the index in strong years (2013, 2017, 2021, 2024), lagged in whipsaw years (2019: +19% against +30%; 2023: +19% against +25%) and helped in 2018 (+1% against −4%) and 2022 (−13% against −17%).

A and C combine: put the US Book's real money in the index under the rule, and let CW2 and Super 30 keep running on paper until they earn a place by beating it. If "maximum CAGR" matters more to you than the 25% limit for this Book, B is the honest answer the data gives.

What building option A takes

  1. An ETF as a Book instrument. config/books.yaml declares each Book's gold ETF and cash fund; an index ETF is a third of the same kind. The Lake fetches its prices with the rest.
  2. A Sleeve that holds it by rule. A strategy's track runs on its market's panels, which leave the Books' ETFs out. Either the index ETF joins the US panels as a security, or the engine gets an index Sleeve beside the gold Sleeve that is in or out by the rule. The second is smaller and keeps the rule in one place.
  3. The usual path to money: a version, the Candidate test against the Book without it, then Paper. The Rule plan, the final check and the Brief need no change.

Things to settle first, which I could not check from here:

  • Which ETF. A US-listed one (VOO, IVV, SPY) is the simplest at moomoo. US-listed funds and shares are US-situs assets: a non-resident's holdings above US$60,000 can fall under US estate tax, and India has no estate-tax treaty with the US. The US Book's US$105,000 in single stocks has the same exposure today. An Ireland-domiciled fund tracking the same index avoids it, but trades in London, which needs a broker that reaches it. Worth a question to a tax adviser before real money goes in.
  • Dividends on a US-listed fund are withheld at source for a foreign holder; the backtest's total-return levels assume they are reinvested whole.
  • Your tax residence. The config assumes an Indian resident's 24 months. If that is not right, the after-tax column changes and so may the ranking of A and B.

Hong Kong

The Hang Seng made 0.9% a year over the range and the trend rule on it made 1.1% to 2.3%, so an index Sleeve is no answer there. The stock Sleeves made 12.2% over the range after the changes and 1.2% in the holdout, under the cash fund's 3%. That is one good window out of two. The recommendation is to leave the Hong Kong Book in Paper and let the Stages decide: nothing needs changing for that, since no Sleeve of it is past Paper. If you want the money working meanwhile, the HK$247,000 could sit in the US Book's index Sleeve, which the Hong Kong Book's gold and cash fund already do in dollars.

8. Still open

  • Taxes are in no backtest. India's short-term gains are taxed at 20%; CW2 with a 10% stop realises most of its gains short-term. A foreign share sold within 24 months is taxed at the slab rate. The after-tax order of the strategies may differ from the pre-tax one.
  • Brokerage is assumed ₹0 at ICICI Direct. If your plan charges for delivery, a strategy turning its Book over several times a year pays it on every trade. Worth checking against a contract note.
  • The order-size guard at 1% of traded value costs India 2.7 points over the range. It is a judgement about your own market impact at the pre-open auction, so it was left alone. Raising it to 3% recovered about half a point; removing it, all of it.
  • A quarantined holding cannot be sold on a strategy's exit. A real 40% fall on a held name is flagged, quarantined, and then only its Disaster stop can sell it. Letting exits through for held names, or having the Health Agent's "real move" verdict lift the Quarantine, would close this.
  • Two-thirds of the India Book is idle on average even after the changes (60%), because the strategies' empty slots and risk-off periods wait in the cash fund. A third Sleeve did not help (25.7% / −16.5% against 25.6% / −18.0% under the loosest settings). An index holding for idle money while the market is in an Uptrend is the obvious thing to test next.
  • A jump flag ages out after 20 sessions. With Quarantines now lifting when their flag goes, a split Yahoo missed that nobody corrected would be released after about a month. A Correction waiting for the security holds the Quarantine, and India's MarketSmith check still catches it; for a liquid US or Hong Kong name the risk is small but real.
  • Five tests fail in ash_new's suite, as they did before this work. Four read this machine's settings.toml and .env (risk-free rates of 6% and 3% where the tests expect 7% and 4%); test_refresh_securities.py expects a security Yahoo has nothing for not to be asked for again.
